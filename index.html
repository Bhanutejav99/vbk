The Sacred Art of the Aduthera

✦ ✦ ✦

At VBK Designs, every stitch is a prayer, and every thread carries a legacy. The Aduthera is not simply a ceremonial fabric—it is a sacred canvas of blessings, meticulously hand-embroidered by master artisans who have preserved traditional craftsmanship across generations.

Using only the finest raw silks, rich satins, and premium gold Zardosi cords, we breathe life into divine motifs. Each curve of a lotus, each curve of a peacock feather, and the sacred geometry of the Swastik are brought to life through hours of meditative needlework, ensuring your most auspicious moments are crowned with timeless elegance.
